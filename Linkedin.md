My cousin was learning to code with AI. Then the internet went down in Kashmir. It's still down.

So I built Cody—an offline AI coding assistant that works everywhere. No internet. No APIs. No credit cards.

**It's not just a chatbot.** It's an autonomous agent:
- Write files, run tests, commit to git—all offline
- Index your own books and documents for semantic search
- Ask it questions about survival manuals, medical texts, whatever you feed it
- Works on a USB stick with open-source models (Qwen for coding, Gemma for reasoning)

The screenshots below show indexing a survival guide and asking real questions offline—and it writing a complete login page on its own.

**For people in places without reliable internet. For developers who need to work offline. For learning without boundaries.**

Open source. Portable. Works everywhere.
